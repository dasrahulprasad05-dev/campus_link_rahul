# Placement Eligibility Criteria

## CGPA Requirements

### Minimum CGPA Cutoffs
- **General Eligibility**: Students must have a minimum CGPA of **6.0** to be eligible for campus placements.
- **Tier-1 Companies** (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe): Minimum CGPA of **7.5** required.
- **Tier-2 Companies** (Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Cognizant): Minimum CGPA of **6.0** required.
- **Product-Based Startups**: Minimum CGPA of **6.5** required, though some may waive this for strong portfolios.
- **Dream Companies** (package > 10 LPA): Minimum CGPA of **7.0** required.
- **Super-Dream Companies** (package > 20 LPA): Minimum CGPA of **8.0** required.

### CGPA Calculation
- CGPA is calculated up to the latest completed semester.
- Supplementary examination results are considered if the exam was completed before the placement drive registration deadline.

## Backlog Policy
- Students with **active backlogs** (any failed subject not yet cleared) are **not eligible** for campus placements.
- Students who had backlogs but have since **cleared all of them** are eligible, provided their CGPA meets the minimum requirement.
- Maximum backlog history allowed: **2 backlogs** (even if cleared). Students with more than 2 historical backlogs may be excluded from Tier-1 drives.

## Branch Eligibility
- **All branches** are eligible for IT/software roles unless the company specifies otherwise.
- **Core engineering roles** (Mechanical at Tata Motors, Electrical at Siemens, etc.) are branch-specific.
- **Cross-branch applications**: Students may apply for roles outside their branch if they meet the skill requirements and the company allows it.

## Year & Graduation
- Only **final-year students** (expected graduation within the current academic year) are eligible.
- Students in extended semesters due to backlogs may participate if they have a confirmed graduation date within 6 months of the drive.

## Registration Requirements
- Students must have a **complete profile** on the placement portal (minimum 80% completion).
- A valid **resume** must be uploaded before the registration deadline.
- Students must have attended at least **one pre-placement training session** to be eligible.
