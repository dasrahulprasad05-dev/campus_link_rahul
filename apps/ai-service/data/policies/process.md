# Placement Drive Process

## Drive Schedule & Timeline

### Pre-Placement Activities (August — September)
1. **Pre-Placement Talks (PPTs)**: Companies present their culture, roles, and expectations. Attendance is mandatory for students who wish to apply to that company.
2. **Profile Completion Deadline**: Students must complete their placement portal profile by **August 31st**.
3. **Resume Review**: Mentors and placement cell review resumes. Students with incomplete or poor-quality resumes are flagged.

### Main Placement Season (September — December)
4. **Drive Registration**: Opens 7 days before each drive. Students register through the placement portal.
5. **Eligibility Filtering**: The system automatically filters students based on CGPA, branch, and backlog criteria.
6. **Aptitude/Online Test**: First round — typically 60-90 minutes covering quantitative, logical, and verbal sections.
7. **Technical Interview**: Second round — assesses technical knowledge, problem-solving, and domain expertise.
8. **HR Interview**: Final round — evaluates communication, cultural fit, and salary expectations.
9. **Results Announcement**: Within 7 days of the drive completion.

### Extended Placement Season (January — March)
10. **Pool Campus Drives**: Multi-college drives for students not yet placed.
11. **Off-Campus Referrals**: Placement cell shares off-campus opportunities.

## Drive Day Protocol

### Reporting
- Students must report at the designated venue **30 minutes before** the scheduled start time.
- Carry: College ID, 2 printed resumes, pen, and government-issued photo ID.
- Dress code: **Formal attire** (no jeans, no sneakers, no casual wear).

### Conduct
- Mobile phones must be **switched off** during aptitude tests and interviews.
- Students found using unfair means (copying, using phones, impersonation) will be **permanently debarred** from all campus placements.
- Students who register for a drive and do not attend without prior intimation will receive a **warning**. Two such incidents result in a **1-drive suspension**.

### Rounds & Progression
- Each round is eliminatory. Only shortlisted students proceed to the next round.
- Wait times between rounds can be 2-4 hours. Students must remain at the venue until released.
- If multiple drives are scheduled on the same day, the placement cell will coordinate to avoid conflicts.

## Communication
- All drive-related communication happens through the **official placement portal** and **registered email**.
- WhatsApp groups are unofficial — students should always verify information on the portal.
- Any discrepancy or grievance should be reported to the Placement Officer within 48 hours.
