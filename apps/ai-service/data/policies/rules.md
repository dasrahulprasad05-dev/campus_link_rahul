# Placement Rules & Offer Policies

## Offer Categories

### Standard Offers (Package < 10 LPA)
- Students who receive a standard offer are **allowed to sit for one more company** if it is classified as a Dream or Super-Dream company.
- If the student receives a higher offer, the standard offer is automatically withdrawn.
- If the student does not receive a higher offer, the standard offer remains binding.

### Dream Offers (Package 10 — 20 LPA)
- Students with a Dream offer **cannot sit for more standard-category drives**.
- Students with a Dream offer **can sit for Super-Dream drives** (package > 20 LPA).
- Only **one Dream offer attempt** is allowed after receiving a standard offer.

### Super-Dream Offers (Package > 20 LPA)
- Students with a Super-Dream offer **cannot sit for any more drives** — they are considered fully placed.
- Super-Dream offers are final and binding.

## Offer Acceptance Rules

### Acceptance Deadline
- Students must accept or decline an offer within **72 hours** of receiving it.
- Failure to respond within 72 hours is treated as acceptance.
- Acceptances are binding — withdrawal after acceptance has penalties (see below).

### Withdrawal Policy
- **Before acceptance**: Students may decline an offer without penalty.
- **After acceptance but before joining**: Students may withdraw with a **written explanation** to the Placement Officer. This counts as one of their allowed attempts.
- **Repeated withdrawals**: A student who withdraws from 2 accepted offers will be **suspended from future campus placement drives** for the remainder of the season.

### Binding Offers
- Once a student accepts a Super-Dream offer, it is **irrevocably binding**.
- Students who renege on a binding offer may face:
  - Removal from the placement portal
  - A negative remark on their placement record
  - The college may inform the company, which could affect the college's relationship with the employer

## Multiple Offers
- A student may hold **at most one active offer** at any time.
- If a student receives a higher-category offer while holding a lower one, the lower offer is **automatically released**.
- Students **cannot hold two offers simultaneously** to compare — they must decide before sitting for the next drive.

## Attendance & Participation

### Mandatory Participation
- Once registered for a drive, attendance is **mandatory**.
- No-shows (without medical certificate or emergency documentation) result in:
  - First offense: Written warning
  - Second offense: 1-drive suspension
  - Third offense: Debarment for the rest of the season

### Pre-Placement Training
- Students must attend at least **75% of scheduled PPT sessions** to maintain eligibility.
- Aptitude training sessions are optional but strongly recommended.

## Grievance & Appeals
- Any grievance related to placement results, drive conduct, or offer disputes must be submitted to the **Placement Grievance Committee** within **7 working days**.
- The committee consists of the Placement Officer, HOD, and a student representative.
- Committee decisions are final and binding.

## Ethical Conduct
- Misrepresentation of CGPA, skills, or project work on the placement portal is a **serious offense** resulting in immediate debarment.
- Sharing aptitude test questions with other students is prohibited and may result in company-wide debarment.
- All placement data, company information, and offer details are **confidential** and must not be shared on social media.
